Caio Barbosa Rodrigues

I am a PhD student at IMPA (Institute for Pure and Applied Mathematics), supervised by Professor Lucas Ambrozio. My thesis defense is expected in March 2027.

My research interests lie in differential geometry and geometric analysis, with a focus on Willmore surfaces, minimal surfaces, and bifurcation theory.

Research

In my preprint, I obtain new symmetric Willmore tori in Berger spheres that bifurcate from the Clifford torus. The proof uses Morse index estimates and bifurcation theory.
